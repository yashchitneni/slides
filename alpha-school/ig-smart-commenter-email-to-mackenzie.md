A few things worth flagging that came up during the design:

**Comment triage is the hard part, not the replies.** An education account with a million followers gets comments about children, enrollment, school philosophy, trolls, and media. The system classifies every comment through a decision tree before anything gets generated. Sensitive topics are hard-skipped. Verified accounts are flagged for you. Alpha School facts only get used if they're pre-approved. The reply generation is the easy part.

**Timing matters more than volume.** Replies in the first 3 hours after posting are what drive algorithmic reach. After 3 days, the bot stops entirely. Replying to old posts looks unnatural and doesn't move the needle.

**This rolls out gradually, not all at once.** Week 1 is Slack approval mode. The bot drafts replies and a human approves before anything posts. Safe categories go auto in week 2. Full auto by week 3, tuned on the edit data from weeks 1-2.
